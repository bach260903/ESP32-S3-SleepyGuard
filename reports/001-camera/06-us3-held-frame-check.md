# US3 held-frame checkpoint (2026-11-20)

- Build: `06-us3-held-frame-build.txt`; flash: `06-us3-held-frame-flash.txt` on CH343 COM5; generated configuration: `06-us3-held-frame-sdkconfig.txt`.
- BOOT_INFO: PSRAM raw 8,388,608 bytes; flash 16,777,216 bytes. PSRAM_TEST passed before CAMERA_INIT. One boot ROM sequence; no reboot in the 32-second capture.
- Worker 1 captured one valid frame, then held the next real framebuffer for 6,000 ms. Measured `fb_get` calls were 282 ms and 68 ms; the six-second hold was a deliberate delay after `fb_get`, not driver blocking time.
- Ordered log markers: `RECOVERY stage=start` → `WORKER_EVENT stop_requested` → `RECOVERY_WAIT` → `fb_returned` → `late_frame_discarded` → `deinit_done result=ESP_OK` → `terminal=STOPPED` → worker 2 `CAMERA_INIT` → `RECOVERY stage=success`.
- Worker 2 returned valid frames after reinit: 15.00, 14.81, 14.81, 14.81 FPS in consecutive five-second reports, each with `invalid=0`.
- The worker stopped about one second after the supervisor requested it, so this capture only exercised the immediate RECOVERY_WAIT report. The five-second repeat and 15-second stop timeout have host boundary tests; a board capture with a longer blocked worker is still needed to observe repeated wait reports.
- T025 board follow-up on 2026-11-21: `06-us3-hold-long.log` uses `CONFIG_BRINGUP_FAULT_HOLD_MS=17000`. Last valid frame at about 1144 ms; stop requested at 5945 ms and RECOVERY start at 6244 ms (about 5100 ms after the last valid observation). `RECOVERY_WAIT` appears at waited_ms 0, 5010, and 10010. The held frame was returned at 17911 ms, then discarded, deinit completed, and STOPPED was emitted before worker 2 initialized. The recovery budget changed from 3 to 2 only when worker 2 started. Worker 2 got a real valid frame and reported 15.00/14.81 FPS with invalid=0. No FRAME_REPORT occurs between the deadline and that valid frame; the first new report spans 5065 ms with 76 valid frames. The two measured fb_get calls were 278 and 67 ms. One boot ROM line, no reboot.
- `06-us3-hold-timeout.log` uses `CONFIG_BRINGUP_FAULT_HOLD_MS=23000`. STOPPING began at 6244 ms, with wait reports at 0, 5010, and 10010 ms. At waited_ms about 15010 the supervisor entered FAULT with `WORKER_STOP_TIMEOUT`. The worker returned the frame, deinitialized, and emitted late STOPPED at about 23917 ms. FAULT reports continued at about 26324, 31324, 36324, 41324, 46324, and 51324 ms; no second CAMERA_INIT or boot ROM line. This demonstrates queue draining after FAULT and no restart on late STOPPED. Host `test_deadline_decision.c` also asserts that late STOPPED yields no start action and leaves FAULT unchanged.
- The 17-second hold was chosen because a 13-second hold would leave only about eight seconds after the five-second no-frame deadline, insufficient for the second five-second repeat. The 23-second hold exceeds the combined five-second frame-loss and 15-second stop-wait boundaries.
- `06-us3-worker-delay.log` is a separate scenario: the real framebuffer was returned before the deliberate 6,000 ms worker delay. Its subsequent frame-loss recovery is not evidence that `fb_get` blocked for six seconds.
- The normal firmware was rebuilt and flashed after the scenario. `CONFIG_BRINGUP_FAULT_INJECTION` is off, fault scenario strings are absent from the application binary, and `06-us3-normal-12s.log` shows valid camera frames with `invalid=0` and no recovery/fault line.
